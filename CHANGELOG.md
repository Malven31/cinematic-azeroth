# Changelog

Each version has a section `## <version> (<date>)` summarising, for players, what is new (`### New`), fixed (`### Fixed`) and changed (`### Changed`). That section, and only that section, is sent to CurseForge as the changelog of the version's file, so it shows on the website and in the CurseForge app. A beta (`1.1.0-beta1`) uses its own section if it has one, otherwise the section of its final version (`1.1.0`). The full feature list is in FEATURES.md.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-16)

First public release of Cinematic Azeroth, for World of Warcraft: Forever.

### New

- One key turns cinematic mode on and off: the whole interface and the nameplates disappear, and black letterbox bars slide in from the top and bottom of the screen. Press it again, or Escape, to come back.
- The interface is hidden by Blizzard's own Toggle UI (the one on Alt+Z), so your key works in combat too.
- Choose what else goes: the names above heads, speech bubbles, combat numbers, and the interaction icons over NPCs and the fishing bobber. Every game setting changed for this is put back exactly as it was afterwards, even after a crash.
- Windows an NPC opens appear alone over the scene: dialog and quests, loot, vendors, flight master, trainers, bank, mail, auction house and more, with their confirmation pop-ups.
- Your bags open over the scene, with your bag keys and at vendors, the bank and the mailbox.
- Quest marks (! and ?) can stay over quest givers.
- With speech bubbles kept, the chat typing bar works in cinematic mode, so you can still talk.
- Cinematic mode can end when combat starts and come back after the fight, and can turn on by itself during flight master flights.
- Letterbox bars: height, buttons for the 2.39:1, 2.00:1 and 1.85:1 film formats on your screen, slide time, and a preview.
- The minimap's mail envelope comes back after the interface has been hidden (the game leaves it hidden until new mail arrives).
- `/cinema debug` and `/cinema watch` record what is on screen, for bug reports.
